# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People and small businesses in and around Kolkata who need to send personal,
professional, or commercial packages domestically and internationally. They
arrive wanting two things fast: confidence that this courier is legitimate and
capable, and a quick way to track a shipment or get in touch. Many visit on
mobile, often in a hurry.

## Product Purpose

Power Media Systems and Services is a Kolkata-based courier and logistics
company offering by-air and by-road delivery, year-round service, on-time
guarantees, and competitive rates. The website is the company's primary
storefront: it must establish credibility, communicate the core services and
value props, surface package tracking, and make contact effortless. Success is
a visitor who trusts the brand and either tracks a parcel or reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Bold, kinetic, dependable. The voice is energetic and direct — speed and
movement made visible. It should feel like a modern logistics startup (fast,
confident, in motion) rather than a sleepy local agency, while still reading as
trustworthy enough to hand over a valuable package.

## Anti-references

- The current template-y Omnifood-derived layout (generic icon-grid boxes,
  flat sections, dated type).
- Sleepy/corporate-stiff logistics sites that feel slow and bureaucratic.
- Cookie-cutter AI landing pages: tracked uppercase eyebrows over every
  section, identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

- **Motion conveys the promise.** Speed and movement are the product; the
  design should feel like it's in motion, not static.
- **Earn trust fast.** Above the fold must answer "are these people legit and
  capable?" within seconds.
- **One job per fold.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, one dominant idea at a
  time — never a wall of equal-weight boxes.
- **Own the orange.** The brand color carries the identity; commit to it rather
  than hedging with neutral safety.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (white text only on saturated
orange fills, never muddy mid-tones). Full keyboard operability and visible
focus states. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with calm fallbacks for every
animation. Mobile-first and fully responsive.
